# TronixRent Semantic Core

## Product reality

TronixRent is a TRON Energy and Bandwidth rental service focused on USDT TRC20 transfers and contract actions. The public messaging is built around very specific user problems, not generic crypto copy.

Verified public positioning from `https://tronix.rent/` and `https://tronix.rent/llms.txt`:

- rent TRON Energy before a USDT TRC20 transfer
- compare TRX burn with Energy rental
- fix `OUT_OF_ENERGY` transfer errors
- handle the `have USDT but no TRX` scenario
- explain why Bandwidth matters together with Energy
- explain `65k` vs `131k` Energy package choice
- expose Smart Router and API-style routing for wallets and tools
